How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself

Routiny isn't something you plan into every morning. You set the shape of your week once, and it fills itself in from there.

1

Set your rhythm

Build a template of a normal week: when you work, train, eat, and sleep. Routiny repeats it automatically, week after week, so you're never starting from a blank day.

2

Adjust up to three days ahead

Something came up? Add a task, move an event, or cancel a block within today, tomorrow, or the day after. Everything further out stays exactly as templated.

3

Get a quiet reminder

Turn on a notification for anything that matters, and hear nothing for the rest. Reminders are optional, event by event.

Built around one idea

Structure that bends, without breaking

Most planners ask you to fill in every day from scratch, or lock you into a rigid recurring calendar. Routiny sits between the two.

Your week has a shape

Define recurring blocks once — the gym on Tuesdays and Thursdays, deep work in the mornings, dinner at eight. Routiny carries that shape into every new week automatically.
